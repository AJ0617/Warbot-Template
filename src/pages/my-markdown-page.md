# About Warbot Template
Warbot Template is a template made for VEX V5 robots

It's straightforward to use, there are comments are there to explain how it works and the best part,
YOU can add any new features or functions that your robot desires.

## Why Warbot Template was made
I, along with you, was also a programmer on my team for VEX High Stakes 24-25, and I didn't really know how to program much.
All I knew how to do was make a 6-motor drivetrain and make some motors move, and that was it. No cool sensor functions
or a perfect autonomous program, just the basics. I also know that not a lot of teams have access to sensors so I want
them to also be able to have a competitive robot.
